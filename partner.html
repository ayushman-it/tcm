THE INDUSTRY GAP

What Problem Are We Solving?

Many educational institutions face similar systemic challenges when updating technical training:

Students complete courses but lack practical hands-on exposure.

Mentors have subject knowledge but need structured curriculum and teaching resources.

Developing new curriculum internally takes significant time and heavy financial resources.

Students lack access to 24×7 learning support outside classroom hours.

Career guidance is often disconnected from day-to-day academic learning.

Institutes lack the technology infrastructure required for modern digital learning.

Employer Expectations Shift: Employers increasingly demand verified skills, practical projects, and real capability rather than traditional certificates alone.

DELIVERABLES

What Do We Provide?

When you partner with TCM One, you get access to 7 integrated ecosystem pillars:

1. Structured Curriculum

Designed around defined learning outcomes: module-wise learning, practical tasks, assignments, projects, assessments, and revision resources.

2. TCM One Platform

Single digital environment for student learning: course content, assignments, assessments, AI assistance, mentor communication, and progress tracking.

3. Ask with AI

Instant explanations when students get stuck. AI provides immediate first-line support without replacing human mentors.

Structured orientation and onboarding for mentors (institute mentors or TCM mentors) covering guidelines, methodologies, and standards.

Students apply what they learn through case studies, real-world projects, problem-solving, and portfolio-oriented work.

6. Career Opportunities

Connects Learning → Skills → Projects → Opportunities (Jobs, internships, career events, skill updates).

7. Student Engagement

Keep students active beyond classroom hours with community participation, experience sharing, and digital learning profile building.

PARTNERSHIP OPTIONS

Our Partnership Models

Choose the operational model that best fits your institution's goals and resources:

MODEL 1

Referral Model

Refer Students. Create Opportunities. Earn Through Referrals.

Designed for institutions that want to introduce TCM One programs to students without taking responsibility for complete course delivery.

  • Low operational involvement
  • Institute promotes & refers students
  • TCM One handles course delivery & AI support
  • Referral benefit on successful enrollments
Ideal for: Colleges, Coaching Centres & Communities testing TCM One.
MODEL 2 · MOST POPULAR

Partnership Model

Build and Deliver TCM One Programs Together

Structured collaboration where the institute and TCM One co-deliver an authorized program to a dedicated student batch (min. 20 students).

  • Choose Institute Mentor or TCM Mentor
  • Structured orientation & mentor training
  • Full access to curriculum, platform & projects
  • Milestone-based commercial structure
Ideal for: Active course partnerships & batch training.
